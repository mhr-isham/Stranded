# Flight School library — MODE-11, 16 November 2026

The three agreed directions are implemented: organize training by skill; keep it optional and directly accessible with chapter recommendations; remember completion/skipping and repeat individual exercises without replaying Basic Training or replacing the current expedition.

The library indexes **38 modules**: 24 existing interactive lessons, 12 guided exercises including Basic Training, and two flight drills. Six groups cover instruments, predictions/uncertainty, navigation/planning, ascent, entry/landing and advanced chapter techniques. Search and skill filtering hide unrelated sections. Every module is available without campaign or training unlocks. Title and expedition-menu entry points open the same library; Campaign setup recommends relevant modules and retains an unconditional chapter-start option subject only to existing Campaign chapter availability.

Opening a lesson records viewing/starting, not completion. The explicit “Got it” action records completion. Skip-for-now is separate, and finished exercises using teaching helpers are labeled completed with help. Earlier completed records are retained when repeating or abandoning a later attempt. These are practice records, not examinations or certifications. Legacy seen-lesson lists are displayed as viewed rather than silently promoted to completed. Training history survives new expeditions, reloads and checkpoint restoration.

Guided exercises reuse bounded portions of the existing tutorial: gravity, horizon dip, planet identification/tracking, year fitting, rover parallax, micrometer, moon fitting, progress sheet, opposition, synodic period and transfer planning, plus full Basic Training. Each begins at its own first step. Focused practice supplies labeled prerequisite observations in an isolated unscored teaching system, leaving the target quantity for the learner. Module completion checks required steps or their recorded completion; skipped required actions cannot silently produce a completed record. Players can explicitly skip an exercise or return with it marked started.

All guided exercises, Basic Training and flight drills preserve the current expedition snapshot and clock. Returning restores its data, mode and context while retaining new training history and seen lessons. Opening the library or a lesson pauses expedition time. The restore guard avoids a closing lesson/library resetting the clock of a newly started practice session. Training does not award an expedition score, Campaign completion or Atlas result. Flight drill completion also records whether ECHO assisted. Existing flight controls, assistance classification and original expedition piloting records remain separate from training records.

Phone testing exposed an existing camp-label overlap when selecting the pendulum in practice. The active guided hotspot is now raised above nearby labels, with its focus marker removed on step changes or tutorial exit. The library uses an opaque, responsive panel to avoid background text interfering with reading.

Validation: **69 tests across 19 files pass**, including coverage of every lesson exactly once, all exercise boundaries, viewed/skipped/completed persistence, recommendations without training gates, restoration of measurements/time with retained learning records, and rejection of incomplete exercise completion. Production build passes with the existing large-bundle warning.

`scripts/check_school.py` passes in Chromium at **390×844 and 1440×900**. It checks all 38 modules, advanced content availability on a fresh Campaign profile, filtering/search, skipped state after reload, viewed versus explicitly completed lessons, repeat access, a guided pendulum exercise using actual controls and explicitly labeled teaching assistance, skipped radius practice, chapter recommendations, optional Campaign start, pausing a live expedition and restoring it after Basic Training. All remaining guided entry points are initialized on desktop, with checks that moon/year/synodic/size prerequisites leave the learner's target measurements unfilled. Screenshots were reviewed. No page errors were observed.

The flight-control and entry-point browser regression scripts also pass with the new library. These tests validate named state transitions and UI paths; they are not complete human playthroughs of every exercise or evidence of engagement across all players. The paired prediction exercises proposed in MODE-12 remain for discussion and are not claimed as implemented here.
