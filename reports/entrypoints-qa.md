# Entry points — MODE-01–04, 15 November 2026

Agreed changes: preserve four distinct entry points and per-chapter Campaign difficulty; unlock completed chapter variations for independent Single Expeditions; make Free Roam direct, unscored and available without Campaign completion, with optional guidance, truth overlay and landing practice. The story and future creative features remain open for discussion.

Single Expedition previews its selected variation and retains random, UTC daily and numeric seeds. Results identify the seed, variation, mode and ruleset revision. New independent expeditions do not advance Campaign; older saves retain their prior progress semantics. Starting scored play explicitly clears sandbox/truth flags.

Free Roam starts at a safe camp with time paused, an identified target and tools accessible. Probe and shadow-stick stations are open without chapter completion; other specialized observations are available by choosing the corresponding system type. Guidance defaults off and suppresses unsolicited companion prompts; enabling it offers existing measurement goals. The truth overlay is labeled and defaults off. Practice landing returns to the same sandbox without a compulsory story sequence. Exploration can be saved and continued, and never awards scored Atlas/achievement/Campaign results.

Validation: 50 tests in 16 files pass. New tests cover variation locks, independent/sandbox Campaign isolation, legacy compatibility and effective guidance rules. `scripts/check_entrypoints.py` exercises phone-width menus, all sandbox system choices, direct entry/tool access, guidance and truth controls, optional landing and continuation, seeded variation selection and switching back to a scored Campaign with its chosen mode. Successful transfer fixtures verify that sandbox debriefs omit scores and sharing, award no Campaign/Atlas/achievements, and return to exploration; independent expedition results enter the Atlas without advancing Campaign. Browser flow reports no page errors. Production build passes with the existing large-bundle warning.
