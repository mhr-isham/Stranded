# Audio refresh and verification

2026-10-29. Original composition: **Error Bars**, 112 BPM, E minor; Em9–Cmaj9–Gmaj9–D6 harmony with alternating melodic phrases. All music and effects are original Web Audio synthesis. No external recordings, licensed samples, streaming services or runtime downloads are used.

## Placement and mix

| Situation | Arrangement / feedback |
|---|---|
| Title, camp and exploration | Melodic synth lead, stereo arpeggios, bass, kick, snare and hats. |
| Precision instruments, sheet and lessons | Reduced arpeggio/pad arrangement, without drums; dry stopwatch ticks remain distinct from the music. |
| Launch planner | Full rhythmic arrangement builds anticipation before committing to flight. |
| Prologue, ascent, transfer, descent and hops | Additional high arpeggios, denser hats and stronger percussion. Continuous engine audio combines filtered noise, low-end thrust and a turbine layer that respond to the flight's thrust/airflow inputs. Transfer burns now also have engine feedback. |
| Story | Sparse harmonic arrangement and reduced music gain keep attention on dialogue. |
| Success / failure | Higher melodic register for success; restrained descending phrase for failure. |
| Tools and rewards | Dry mechanical switches/ticks; measuring chirp for hill/shadows; double shutter for sky/parallax images; radio acquisition for transfer correction; rising chords for discoveries; separate launch, parachute and touchdown cues. |

The first render had excessive engine-to-music contrast. Engine noise/sub levels were reduced, music was raised, and pad envelopes gained a sustained body. Defaults are 65% music / 80% effects. Independent sliders allow music-only or effects-only use. Old saves use these defaults without migration. The shared master controls every voice, including scheduled sounds. A compressor and subsonic filter protect the mix; duplicate rapid reward cues are limited.

## Verification

- `npm test`: **45 tests in 14 files pass**, including scene selection, percussion removal during precision work/story, flight energy and bounded note generation.
- `npm run build`: passes. The existing large-bundle warning remains; this refresh adds roughly 8 kB to the minified JavaScript, with no audio asset payload.
- `python scripts/check_audio.py`: Chromium Web Audio renders complete harmony cycles for exploration, focus, flight, story, success and failure, every one-shot cue, continuous engine, and combined flight stress mixes. It checks for nonfinite samples, silence and clipping, and writes WAV previews and metrics.
- Combined flight, engine, launch and discovery output peaks at **0.545** at default volumes and **0.696** with both sliders at 100% (full scale is 1.0). Neither render has clipped or nonfinite samples. These figures describe this deterministic stress render, not every possible mix.
- Live browser checks cover no audio before a gesture; shared mute and re-enable; independent music/effects volumes; background suspension/resume; one AudioContext rather than duplicate contexts; bounded active voices; persisted sliders after reload; and graceful operation without Web Audio support.
- The actual transfer component runs through its animation and into target entry with the new engine cues. Desktop (1440×900) and phone-sized (390×844) audio controls were exercised and screenshots inspected. The browser run reports no JavaScript page errors.

The measurements establish output integrity and functional placement; they do not establish a universal subjective preference. Headphone/phone-speaker listening and Safari/iOS autoplay behavior still merit real-device feedback. The previews generated by the script make that comparison repeatable without committing large audio files to the game.
